Cel Shader

March 2024

A cel shading/toon shading practice.

The development group consisted of me as the programmer, the 3D artist, and the game mechanics designer, as well as a few friends as the 2D artist, the level designer and the music artist.

The art style (and the title) of the game were inspired by the Monument Valley, featuring a non-photorealistic, flat shading of the environment.
